# History and groups

History contains scanned, imported, manually entered and created codes. Open an entry to see its content, barcode type, timestamps, duplicate detections and user-selected device label.

Use **Groups** in the bottom action bar to create, rename or delete groups. Empty groups remain available and can synchronize through iCloud. To move an entry, tap its three-dot menu, choose **Move to group**, then select the destination. QR Werk deliberately uses this consistent menu instead of drag-and-drop.

Bookmarks complement groups and have their own history segment.

Deleting an entry first moves it to **Trash**. In the Trash segment, restore it or delete it permanently. **Empty Trash** permanently removes every trashed item after confirmation. With iCloud enabled, trash and restorations synchronize between the user's devices.

Use the direct **Export** button to export visible entries, selected groups or individual codes.
