# Settings

## QR and scan settings
Configure duplicate handling, the pause between scans, autofocus, scan filters, QR colors, error correction and the optional outer frame. A one-second scan pause and autofocus are recommended defaults.

## Data management
Automatic logging stores scanned, imported and created values in History. Storage is unlimited. Optional iCloud Sync synchronizes history, groups, bookmarks and duplicate detections between your Apple devices. Backup and restore manage a complete local data file; CSV export is intended for tables.

## Appearance
Choose System, Light, Dark or Black and select a standard accent color or your own color. Language options are German, English, French and Italian.
