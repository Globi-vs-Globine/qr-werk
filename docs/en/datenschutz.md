# Privacy and open source

QR Werk recognizes camera frames and imported images locally with Apple's AVFoundation and Vision frameworks. Scanned content is not uploaded to Apple, Google or another recognition service. The app contains no advertising, analytics or user tracking and does not require a QR Werk account.

Optional iCloud synchronization is off by default. If enabled, history, groups, bookmarks, duplicate detections, trash and the user-selected device label are stored in the user's private CloudKit database. External connections occur only through optional iCloud or an action the user deliberately starts, such as opening a URL, searching or sharing.
