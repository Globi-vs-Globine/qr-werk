# Privacy and open source

QR Werk analyses camera images and imported photos locally with Apple AVFoundation and Vision. Scan content is not sent to Apple, Google or another recognition service. There is no QR Werk account, advertising, analytics or tracking service.

Optional iCloud Sync is disabled by default. When enabled, history, groups, bookmarks and duplicate detections are stored in your private iCloud. External connections occur only when you invoke an action such as opening a URL, searching, sharing online or enabling iCloud.

QR Werk is based on Simple QR by Tom Fong and remains licensed under GPL-3.0. See `PRIVACY.md` and `LICENSE` for the authoritative documents.
